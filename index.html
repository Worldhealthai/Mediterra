The
Artemisium Strait

One of the most powerful maritime corridors in the Mediterranean.

Strong natural currents renew the water continuously, delivering exceptional oxygen levels and a rich mineral flow that shapes our mussels' character. This is not a location chosen for convenience — it is a place with its own memory.

  • Continuous natural water movement
  • High dissolved oxygen levels
  • Rich mineral and nutrient flow
  • Stable, balanced ecosystem
03

The Method

I

Long-Line Cultivation

Suspended in the strait's moving water column, mussels develop through entirely natural filtration — no artificial feeding, no shortcuts.

II

Daily Oversight

Water conditions monitored every day. Temperature, salinity, oxygen flow — nothing is left to assumption.

III

Selective Harvest

Harvested only at peak maturity. Each batch assessed for size, purity, and shell integrity before leaving the water.

IV

Clean Dispatch

Processed and delivered to Athens within 24 hours. Cold chain maintained from strait to market, without compromise.
