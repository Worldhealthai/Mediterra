The place

Why we farm
in the strait.

The Artemisium Strait runs between North Evia and the mainland. It has one of the strongest currents in this part of the Aegean, so the water around our lines is replaced all day and all night.

The current renews the water around our lines around the clock. The mussels never sit in still water.

Moving water carries more dissolved oxygen, which helps mussels grow firm, full and clean.

The strait brings minerals and plankton past the lines. The mussels filter it themselves. We add nothing.

The area is stable and protected, and ropes hung in open water suit it well.

Stage 01

Long-line cultivation

Young mussels grow on ropes hung in open water. They feed by filtering the sea, so there is no artificial feed.

Stage 02

Daily checks

We check the water every day: temperature, salinity and oxygen.

Stage 03

Selective harvest

We lift the lines when the mussels are mature and check each batch for size, cleanliness and shell condition.

Stage 04

Dispatch

Cleaned, packed and in Athens within 24 hours, kept cold the whole way.
