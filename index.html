The Artemisium
Strait

One of the Mediterranean’s most powerful marine corridors. A body of water with its own memory.

Strong, continuous currents deliver oxygen and minerals directly to our long-line arrays — creating the precise conditions that define our mussels’ character. This is not a location chosen for convenience.

  • Continuous natural water renewal
  • Exceptional dissolved oxygen levels
  • Rich mineral and nutrient profile
  • Stable, protected ecosystem

02  ·  The Method

Sea-crafted precision,
from water to door.

01

Long-Line Cultivation

Suspended in the moving water column. Natural filtration only — no artificial feeding, no shortcuts, no compromise.

02

Daily Oversight

Temperature, salinity, and oxygen levels monitored every day. Nothing is left to assumption.

03

Selective Harvest

Harvested only at peak maturity. Each batch assessed for size, purity, and shell integrity before leaving the water.

04

Clean Dispatch

Delivered to Athens within 24 hours. Cold chain maintained from strait to door, without exception.
