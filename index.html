The
Artemisium Strait

One of the Mediterranean’s most powerful marine corridors.

Strong natural currents renew the water continuously — delivering oxygen, minerals, and the precise conditions that shape our mussels’ character. This is not a location chosen for convenience. It is a place with its own memory.

  • Continuous natural water movement
  • High dissolved oxygen levels
  • Rich mineral and nutrient flow
  • Stable, balanced ecosystem
03

The Method

Sea-crafted precision across every stage — from the water column to your door.

I

Long-Line
Cultivation

Suspended in the strait’s moving water column. Natural filtration only — no artificial feeding, no shortcuts.

II

Daily
Oversight

Water conditions monitored every single day. Temperature, salinity, oxygen — nothing is left to assumption.

III

Selective
Harvest

Harvested at peak maturity only. Each batch assessed for size, purity, and shell integrity before leaving the water.

IV

Clean
Dispatch

Delivered to Athens within 24 hours. Cold chain maintained from strait to market, without exception.

“This is not mass aquaculture. This is sea-crafted precision.”
