Operational Knowledge

I

Preparation & Cleaning

Rinse under cold running water. Remove beards by pulling firmly toward the hinge. Discard any shells that remain open after tapping — they are not alive. Use within 24 hours of delivery for optimal quality.

II

Cooking Techniques

Mussels require minimal cooking — 3 to 5 minutes in a covered pan with white wine, garlic, and herbs is sufficient. They are done the moment the shells open. Overcooking reduces texture and flavour significantly.

III

Nutritional Profile

A 100g serving provides approximately 172kcal, 24g of protein, and significant omega-3 fatty acids, vitamin B12, zinc, and iron. Among animal proteins, mussels carry the lowest carbon footprint per gram of protein.

IV

Storage & Cold Chain

Store live mussels in the coldest part of your refrigerator, covered with a damp cloth — never sealed in an airtight container. They require air circulation to remain alive. Consume within 2–3 days of delivery.
