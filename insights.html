Operational Knowledge
I

Preparation & Cleaning

Rinse under cold running water. Remove beards by pulling toward the hinge. Discard shells that remain open after tapping. Use within 24 hours of delivery for optimal flavour.

II

Cooking Techniques

3 to 5 minutes in a covered pan with white wine, garlic, and herbs. Done the moment the shells open. Overcooking ruins texture — remove from heat immediately.

III

Nutritional Profile

100g delivers 24g protein, omega-3 fatty acids, vitamin B12, zinc, and iron. Among animal proteins, mussels carry the lowest carbon footprint per gram — by a significant margin.

IV

Storage & Cold Chain

Store live mussels covered with a damp cloth — never sealed airtight. They need air circulation. Consume within 2–3 days of delivery. Keep in coldest part of refrigerator.
